The Regional-Stat System

A game with no hit points. Six stats double as the durability of a body region, so every landed blow lands somewhere — and what it costs you is measured in what you can still do.

If you have never seen this before

Most games give you a pool of hit points. You subtract from it, and nothing changes until it hits zero — a fighter on their last point swings exactly as well as one at full health. This system refuses that.

Here, your six stats are your body. Three of them are Structure — Constitution is your head and chest, Brawn is your arms, Drive is your legs — and each one is both how hard that region is to break and how much punishment it has left. The other stats are Faculty: how hard you are to hit in the first place.

So a blow to the leg is rolled against the leg, graded against how sturdy that leg still is, and paid for out of that same leg. Cut deep enough and you do not lose a number — you lose your footing, your reach, your grip on the sword. Only the head and chest running out kills you. Everything else just makes you worse at fighting, which is usually enough.

The Codex's How it works tab explains all of it from scratch, with no jargon and no shorthand.
